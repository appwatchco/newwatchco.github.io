6A. Accountability and Competitions

Accountability buddies are for mutual encouragement, not competitive ranking. Competitions are optional habit challenges. ScreenTame does not collect stakes, hold wagers or pay competition winnings; scores are not a payment or prize entitlement.

Connections and limits: connect and enable every required app, checking the actual app displayed by Apple's selector rather than only a saved label. During a competition, the lower daily-open limit and shorter session length each apply independently. For example, personal limits of 10 opens × 3 minutes and competition limits of 4 opens × 5 minutes give 4 opens × 3 minutes, not a pooled daily time allowance. Joining does not relax personal limits. Personal and competition streaks are separate.

Forfeits: leaving a competition or turning off/removing required protection during it counts as a forfeit in versions supporting these checks. In last-person-standing mode, exceeding a competition open limit also eliminates you. An offline or missing record alone is not treated as proof of a loss. Detection and sharing depend on the device recording and syncing the change; the app is not a guaranteed anti-cheating system.

Results: in versions supporting fixed-date final results (1.0.1 build 40 and later), scoring stops at the end-date cutoff. Once all participants' final scores are available, eligible participants are ranked by fewest total competition opens, then longest completed competition streak. Equal scores share first place; if no eligible participant remains there is no winner. A partial final day contributes its opens but does not earn an early completed streak day. Last-person-standing competitions can finish when one eligible participant remains and the required records confirm the outcome, or with no winner if everyone is eliminated.

Sync and alerts: current-day opens can appear before the day's streak is awarded. Another person's scores reflect their latest upload; refreshing cannot retrieve activity their device has not synced. Shields may show cached summaries. At a fixed deadline, the app processes closure on refresh and removes active competition limits, returning to personal settings. Final results and result alerts can wait for final scores, including from closed/offline devices. Notifications require permission and depend on Apple delivery and background execution; they are not guaranteed to arrive immediately, at midnight, or at the exact deadline. Keep both devices updated, open ScreenTame and refresh Friends if results are pending. Older competitions without a verified cutoff may not have a final result.

Leaving and reporting: leaving records a forfeit rather than erasing the competition history. Use Report / block on the leaderboard to report a problem or hide a participant on your device; blocking does not change scores or remove that person for others. Contact james@newwatchco.com for support or deletion requests. Ending a competition or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete its cloud records.
